Overview 
 
Offering high-performance, high port density, and low latency, CloudEngine 6800 series switches enable enterprises and carriers alike to build 
cloud-oriented data center networks. The series features an advanced hardware design combined with either 10 GE, 25 GE, or 50 GE access 
ports, and 40 GE, 100 GE, or 200 GE uplink ports. Advanced data center features, high-performance stacking technologies, and flexible airflow 
capabilities complete the series. CloudEngine 6800 is well-suited to both the core and aggregation layers, and is fully compatible with 
CloudEngine 16800 and 12800 series switches, enabling enterprises to build scalable, simplified, open, and secure networks.
