# Rejected left-face history and repair v2

The original dedicated left built-in imagegen call is preserved as qa/imagegen-raw/left-chroma.png and qa/imagegen-alpha/left.png. It had five long rail recesses per row and was correctly rejected. The subsequent choice to mirror the right generation into views/left.png was itself an immediate REWORK violation. That full invalid delivery is preserved under repair-v2/before/ and is not part of the repaired lineage.

Repair v2 generated physical-left independently from the official Juniper Left view photo, an exact JNP304/MX304 physical-left real photograph, a target-configuration real photo and the official page-99 geometry diagram. right.png, the old mirrored left, old GLBs and all AI/render derivatives were explicitly forbidden inputs.

Three repair attempts are recorded in repair-v2/qa/imagegen-manifest.csv. The selected first repair attempt has the correct 2 x 4 recesses, front-right/rear-left orientation, large front pad and left-specific nonuniform screw/black-locator sequence. Two ratio/factual retries were rejected. To meet physical ratio without whole-face axis stretching, only feature-free gray metal gaps were lengthened; mechanical feature spans remained unstretched, followed by one uniform resize.

The accepted independent output is views/left.png. non-mirror-audit.json proves distinct left/right texture hashes, positive identity transforms, no negative/mirrored nodes, and correct asymmetric UV landmarks.
