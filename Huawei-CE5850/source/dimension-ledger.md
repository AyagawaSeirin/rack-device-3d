Interpretation:

- 442 mm is treated as the sheet-metal chassis width because official product photos without brackets match that body and the detachable rack hardware mounts to side holes.
- 420 mm is the chassis depth of the complete appliance; no cable-management arm or shipping dimension is used.
- 43.6 mm is the actual chassis height and confirms 1U.
- The two AC inlet faces and fan handles are nearly flush and are represented within the 420 mm main envelope, with only source-visible latch relief added.
- Overall bracket width is tracked separately and never used to distort the six body textures.
